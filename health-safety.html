Removing people from the danger zone

Traditional refractory demolition puts crews inside confined, hot, and structurally unpredictable vessels. Our remote robotic methods change that entirely: operators command the machine from a safe distance, so no one is exposed to falling linings, dust, or heat. On a decade of confined space demolition, that has meant zero lost-time injuries.

Planned like an operation, not a task

Every shutdown is surveyed, sequenced, and staged before work begins. Exclusion zones are established and enforced, the shell is protected throughout, and each scope ends with a verified surface handed back to the reline crew — safely and on schedule.
