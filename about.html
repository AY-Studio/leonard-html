How we work

Planning. Organisation. Preparation. Installation.

01

Planning

Every shutdown is surveyed and sequenced before a machine arrives on site, so the scope, the risks, and the schedule are understood in advance.

02

Organisation

Trained, registered crews and the right equipment are mobilised around your outage window, coordinated with your plant team from the outset.

03

Preparation

Exclusion zones are established, the shell is protected, and access is staged — removing our people, and yours, from the danger zone.

04

Installation

Robotic demolition and precision refractory installation, ending with a verified surface handed back to the reline crew, on schedule.

Safety-driven from the start

Leonard was established over 30 years ago on a simple conviction: that the most dangerous work in heavy industry — demolishing and relining hot, confined, structurally unpredictable vessels — should not put people at risk. That belief still drives every method we engineer today, and it is why we invested early in remote robotic systems that take crews out of harm's way.

A global reach, a specialist focus

From our base in Cookstown, County Tyrone, we deliver projects across North America, Europe, Africa, the Middle East, and South East Asia. We stay deliberately specialist — refractory installation, robotic arm and confined space demolition, and media grading — so that the depth of our expertise is never in question.

Trusted by demanding industries

Some of the world's largest cement, lime, aluminium, and energy producers rely on Leonard for their most critical shutdowns.
