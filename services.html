How we work

Principal contractor, or trusted partner

How we engage depends on what the job needs. Sometimes we lead the programme end to end; sometimes we bring our specialist capability alongside your existing providers. Either way, the responsibility for a safe, on-time result is shared and clear.

As principal contractor

We take full responsibility for the scope — planning, safety, sequencing and delivery — and hand back a verified surface, on schedule. One point of accountability for the whole shutdown.

In partnership

We integrate with your own teams or a lead contractor, supplying the robotic demolition and refractory expertise that complements the wider programme — without duplicating what already works.
