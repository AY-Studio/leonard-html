Metal Processing industry solutions

Leonard provides specialist refractory maintenance, robotic demolition, and industrial services to the metal processing industry, supporting the safe and efficient operation of high-temperature processing facilities.

Challenges

Mastering extreme heat, abrasion, and unrelenting production demands

Cement manufacturing punishes equipment. We engineer maintenance solutions that withstand the inferno inside your kiln and the relentless wear in your mills.

  • Thermal shock resistance

    Our refractory installations combat spalling and cracking caused by rapid temperature cycling, extending lining life in your preheater and cooler.

  • Abrasive material handling

    We apply wear-resistant linings and precise media grading to protect chutes, ducts, and mills from raw meal and clinker abrasion.

  • Continuous process integrity

    Our robotic demolition and rapid installation teams execute shutdown scopes that minimise lost production hours and restore thermal efficiency.
